Coverage & reading guide

“Listed” means an official page supports the entry. It does not establish that an award is still funded or that applications are open. Announcement, renewal and historical dates are distinguished in each entry.

NSF Q-SEnSE and RQS belong to the Quantum Leap Challenge Institutes. NIST partnerships are a separate relationship. MRSEC and CIQM predate the 2018 National Quantum Initiative. CIFAR is a Canadian-based international network; EPiQS is philanthropic.

Each entity has one home page. Program links connect centers to their funding or coordination structure. The directory is independent of the publication datasets in the other maps.

A RESEARCH DIRECTORY, NOT A RANKING

Quantum materials is one part of a wider quantum ecosystem. Each entry explains whether its connection is materials research, enabling infrastructure, education or a neighboring area of quantum science.

Dates describe the cited event, not necessarily the first use of a name or the start of every award. See the source and status notes before treating an entry as a current funding opportunity.
