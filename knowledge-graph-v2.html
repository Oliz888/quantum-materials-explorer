SCOPE / EVIDENCE / VERSION

A reproducible collection, with boundaries.

What is included

Five Nature subject channels, all Nature Portfolio journals, 2010–2026, using Nature’s research/reviews/protocols search filters. News and comment are outside this query. 2026 is partial through 4 November.

The classification graph retains 39 selected nodes and 47 official broader–narrower links. Only the five selected ontology channels have a paper collection here. Other nodes and the separate citation-topic scheme are not assigned invented counts.

How completeness is checked

Nature displays at most 1,000 results per query. Each channel is therefore queried separately by year. Page ranges, unique article URLs, source years and yearly totals are reconciled before a collection is marked complete.

One article can have several subject memberships. Each membership keeps its own official query URL and page number. Counts across channels overlap and must not be added as unique papers.

What counts do not establish

This is a snapshot of Nature’s subject index, not all quantum-materials research. Broad channels include other research. Changes in journal coverage and subject tagging affect apparent growth; a zero indexed count is not a field’s birth date.

Paper–topic links record classification membership, not paper-to-paper citations or causal influence. Material, phenomenon, property and method labels are our overlapping reading aids.
