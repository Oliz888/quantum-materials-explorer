SOURCE NOTES

What this map can tell you

One host, multiple roles

A university can host education programs and research centers. A center can have several hosts. Its entity is counted once in the directory; each recorded host receives a participation count. A joint host is not automatically a grant recipient.

A reproducible selection

The colors summarize the evidence collected here. Changing weights changes the emphasis. They do not measure scientific quality, all laboratories at an institution, or its complete funding history. Research-only laboratories can be compared using the research or funding view.

Money with provenance

Amounts use NSF’s fundsObligatedAmt field, a cumulative award obligation as retrieved. Each unique award ID is counted once, for its named prime recipient only. Partner shares, actual expenditures, inflation and award amendments over time are not reconstructed.

All selected funding records & official sources

Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau, via DOE NETL. Locations: linked institutional sources. Map labels and state positions are cartographic approximations.
