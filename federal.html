Coverage, sources & interpretation

Research centers and themes are included alongside formal study programs, with their actual type shown. A center listing does not imply a degree or credential. Cross-listing preserves one entity rather than creating a duplicate institution.

Lines in the overview indicate the stated program links, shared hosts or cross-listing. They do not measure collaboration or influence. Map markers are approximate host cities, not exact laboratory coordinates or every partner site.

An official listing does not establish that an award is still funded or applications are open. Each entry distinguishes dated announcements, renewals and historical programs. The institutional directory is independent of the publication datasets.
