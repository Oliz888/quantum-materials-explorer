The starting review, Tokura, Kawasaki & Nagaosa (2017), has five subject tags. Its sixth related link is a separate citation topic, not another Nature ontology subject.

Annual totals and paper previews

85 official annual query counts cover five channels × 2010–2026. The last year ends on 4 November. Individual paper previews are incomplete; they are not used to estimate annual totals. Other nodes and the separate citation topic have no annual collection. No abstracts or article full texts are republished.

Growth is not influence

Yearly output and cumulative output describe publication activity within a specified channel. They do not by themselves establish field maturity, impact, citation flow or which discipline came first. Historical tagging changes and changing journal coverage also affect comparisons.

Paper–topic links mean a record appeared in an official channel or had an explicit article tag. No paper-to-paper citation network has been collected.

Official sources and collection rules
  • Nature subject index and how subject tagging works.
  • Nature search guidance: dates, journals and article types define the search scope.
  • Hierarchy: explicit “Browse broader subjects” / “Browse narrower subjects” links on official subject pages. The /nphys pages supply hierarchy evidence; their article previews are Nature Physics only.
  • The separate Nature Index topic reports trends across publications. Those numbers are not used as Nature channel totals here.
  • Records deduplicate by canonical Nature article URL. Memberships are retained separately. Missing memberships in this incomplete sample do not mean a paper lacks those subjects.
  • Counts are captured on 4 November 2026, not live. Each annual query uses one subject and the research/reviews/protocols article-type filter, across all journals, with the same start and end year. Links and displayed result-count evidence are included in the downloadable snapshot.
  • 2010–2026 sums match each channel’s captured all-time total. This is a property of the current index, not proof that the field started in 2010. An official zero result is distinct from an unknown value and cannot establish that no related research existed.
  • Cumulative counts start at 2010. The year control affects only the history panel; graph badges show captured all-time totals. Shared-subject totals are not additive. 2026 is year-to-date and should not be compared directly with a full calendar year.
